## Full description (both stores, 4000 chars max)

Welcome to the bayou. Blood in the Bayou is an open-world action game from Level 29 Entertainment, set in a Gulf Coast city overrun by the undead and run by hustlers.

EXPLORE AN OPEN WORLD
Cruise from the swamps and mountains to the Gulf of America coast. Visit downtown, the university and Jester Stadium, the Santa Monica Pier with its rides, and Ronniebaby's penthouse high above the city.

FIGHT TO SURVIVE
Zombies and gators roam the streets and swamps. Gear up, take them down, and shoot birds out of the sky. Take a seat in the .50 cal gunner position of the bomber and clear the streets from above.

WORK YOUR WAY UP
Pick up jobs across the city, earn tips for fast work, build streaks, and earn S to C shift grades. Climb the ranks for faster work and tougher vehicles. Head out on the Natchez-style steamboat and net shrimp and fish in the Gulf.

BOOMCITY CASINO
Try your luck at blackjack, poker, roulette and six Level 29 Jester slot machines, all with in-game money only. Every spin feeds the Level 29 Jackpot.

RIDE IN STYLE
Visit Bonnie De Vette's dealership for lowriders on chrome spokes, Houston-slab style.

PLAY WITH FRIENDS
Shoot hoops 2v2 on a court enemies can't enter, hit the club, and tip your friends while they dance.

Casino games use virtual in-game currency only. There is no real-money gambling and no prizes of real value. Ages 17+.
