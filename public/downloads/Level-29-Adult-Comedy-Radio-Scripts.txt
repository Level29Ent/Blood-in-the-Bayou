RECORDING NOTES
Use original announcer voices—not celebrity impressions. Read with relaxed,
confident comic timing. Record each filename separately. Leave one second of
silence at both ends; deliver clean WAV masters plus MP3 copies. No copyrighted
music is supplied. Timing estimates depend on delivery. These are scripts,
not recorded audio; they are not connected to radio rotation yet.

Mix recipe: music track → short station ID → music track → one commercial →
DJ bumper → music track. Do not stack all commercials back-to-back. Keep speech
clear over a quiet, licensed/original bed. Pause radio speech for mission dialogue.
No real prices, websites, endorsements or checkout destinations are implied.
Everything advertised below is an in-game fictional service or studio promo.
