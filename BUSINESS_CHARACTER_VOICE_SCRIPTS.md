# Business Character Voice Recording Pack

Record **one MP3 per row**, named exactly as listed. Mono, 44.1 kHz, clean/dry vocal; trim dead air at both ends without cutting breaths or consonants. Avoid music, reverb and clipping. Record two takes if you want options, but deliver the chosen take with the exact filename. Use your own voice or an actor who has agreed to perform the role.

Stage finished files in `public/game/business-vo/` or send the completed recordings together as a ZIP. **These new business filenames are a recording plan, not automatically wired audio triggers.** After upload, the clips still need to be connected and timing-tested. Existing Ronniebaby clips remain in `public/game/rbvo/` and are unchanged. The optional roux handoff is a new business-voice file, not a replacement for his original script.

Cues describe where a line should eventually play; do not read the cue or filename aloud. Jojo’s scene currently uses fixed animation beats: provide a short, energetic take for the six-second gumbo statement and a separate “Not in my house!” clip. Longer takes may require scene retiming when integrated. Damion’s jokes need space for a reaction; his existing browser speech is only a placeholder, not a recorded imitation. General staff roles can share an actor across branches; named characters should have distinct delivery.
