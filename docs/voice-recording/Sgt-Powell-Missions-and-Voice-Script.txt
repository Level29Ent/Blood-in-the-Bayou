SGT. POWELL — EXPANDED MISSIONS & VOICE RECORDING SCRIPT
Blood in the Bayou · Level 29 Military Base

STATUS
This is a proposed mission roster and recording script, not confirmation that these missions or recordings are implemented in the game.

VOICE DIRECTION
Firm, composed and encouraging. Speak clearly rather than shouting every line.
Record each named line as a separate audio file. Leave a short silence before and after each take.

MISSION RULES FOR IMPLEMENTATION
- Show a waypoint for every destination, resource, boss, survivor and return point.
- Unlock missions in order; allow a failed mission to be retried.
- Award fictional in-game cash only, once per completed run.
- Airborne mission flights last 60 seconds. The regular paid ride remains 29 seconds.
- Airborne shots require actual aimed hits on visible targets; no proximity kills.
- Mission availability, targets, rewards and timers below are proposed design values.
