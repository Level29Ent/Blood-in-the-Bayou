# Enterprise challenge and outer-coast expansion

## Economy (earned game cash only)

Generic purchasable businesses now quote $25,000 below22,000sq world units, $40,000 below45,000, or $65,000 for larger footprints. Every existing business adds15% to the next quote, capped at +150%; quotes round to $500. The display and purchase debit use the same helper. Saved business keys, accrued balance, checkpoint, safehouse deeds and existing ownership are retained. Current owners pay nothing retroactively; income stays $150/minute/business with the existing30-minute offline accrual cap. No mortgages or real-money purchases are added.

## Repeatable contracts

Pause → Enterprise Contracts is available in solo play; campaign/shift/story/city/street/dispatch/heist conflicts block acceptance.

- Merchant Supply Circuit:3 stopped delivery checkpoints in a non-boat vehicle, then on-foot USE at dispatch. $2,400 base.
- Waterfront Cleanup:4 visible litter pickups on the relocated beach (safe nearby approach fallback). Tap USE and stay on foot at each pile for4 seconds, or hold U. $1,800 base.
- Closing-Time Defense:3 waves of4 zombies, capped by the existing live population; no zoo objectives. Clear each wave, then return. $4,200 base.

All run on active-play time, fail on death/timeout, pay once only after return, and impose an active-play cooldown before another contract. Existing business owners get20% more contract pay. Canceled/failed contracts do not pay. They are separate repeatable activities, not replacements for the210-mission campaign. Pickup objects reuse shared geometry/materials and unload on city rebuild. No new gangs, hostile police, GLB imports or per-frame texture allocations.

## Map

The broad inner western bayou is narrowed toward the outer512world-unit strip, while existing boat/dock basins and horizontal access corridors are protected. Reserved properties, roads/bridges, attractions and inhabited parcels are not reclaimed. Some water deliberately remains at working docks and protected landmarks; this is not removal of every pond.

A16-tile southern extension holds Sunset Beach beyond the existing highway, connected by a graded pedestrian boardwalk. Existing city coordinates and the old road network remain fixed. The boardwalk is sidewalk, not a new disconnected vehicle road. Shore surf, sharks, lifeguard equipment and fishing logic follow the beach object; old inland beach water becomes dry ground. The western foam/fog alignment follows the new outer bayou. Existing boat basins are not teleported or filled. Saved coordinates remain valid; old inland swimming positions now rest on land.

Infill ceiling16→24 parcels, retaining dry-lot/entrance/protection checks. Actual count can be lower if no safe parcel exists; new bakery/cycle-shop labels are available, but only safely placed shops exist. Homes are scenery exteriors and infill shops reuse generic property services. Intentional parks remain open.
