# City playability pass

## Player-visible changes

- The local outfit preview and third-person character now use `makeJester` (the existing grinning Mardi Gras Jester), not a generic NPC. It reuses the live clothed rig across changes, preserving limb animation and camera/weapon ownership. Default hat and ruff return when resetting; equipped headwear hides the default hat and equipped tops hide the ruff to avoid double layers. It does not replace NPC identities.
- Buy once and equip locally owned garments free: tops $175+, pants $150+, footwear $125+, headwear $75+, accessories $200+; cut adds $25 per tier. Existing selected garments are grandfathered. Sheldon’s and Bayou Fashion offer all five categories; Shoes offers footwear; Sporting Goods and Tactical Surplus offer training categories; Luxury and Jewelry offer accessories/headwear. No real-money purchases. Use the existing store service input **U** while inside the correct room/floor or the Local Counter button. Use **WEAR IT · OUTFIT VIEW / C** to see the outfit in third person. Clothes remain equipped in first person but that view intentionally hides the full body.
- Local ownership and selection persist in browser storage, campaign resume, save-code export and residence-store export/import. If storage is unavailable the current session still applies the outfit and warns. Multiplayer packets carry only validated catalog selection IDs; remote Jester rigs update on an outfit-stamp change, not every frame. This is cosmetic peer synchronization, not a server-authoritative anti-cheat economy. Networked multi-device play is unverified.
- The riverboat’s 16 slot props and four tables have reachable per-machine/per-table actions: six existing slot themes, blackjack, five-card Jacks-or-Better draw poker and single-zero roulette. The center action and Casino controls still expose the same games. Actions are not shown on upper decks or while outside the boarded boat. Card wagers cannot be replaced or doubled-paid by restarting a live hand; stakes, game IDs and selections are validated. Blackjack uses a finite 52-card deck and settles dealer naturals correctly; exact-number roulette bets on zero now pay correctly. Fictional game currency only. Closing a card panel retains the unfinished hand in this page session; a page reload discards an unfinished hand and its already-deducted stake. Winnings and deducted game cash checkpoint through the existing save system, subject to browser storage.
- At Blackwater Football Stadium, use **U** at the service point to choose a five-attempt field-goal or target-passing timing drill. Each successful attempt pays $250 × attempt number. Repeated input is debounced; completed attempts cannot pay twice. This is a solo timing challenge, not a simulated full football match, rendered ball physics, teams or opponents. Jester Stadium retains its existing field-goal drill with corrected total earnings.
- **NOLA Businesses → Interactive City Map** now searches all registered named building entrances and mall shop names. Results include approximate district labels, mall floor and distance. Selecting a shop directs to the mall’s safe **street entrance**, not a nonexistent ground-floor copy of its upstairs room. The casino routes to the harbor boarding entrance, not its off-map interior. Waypoints remain direct bearings, not turn-by-turn driving directions, and never teleport the player or override active job/mission guidance.
- Inside businesses, **LOCAL COUNTER / ACTIVITIES** exposes existing $1000 vehicle-delivery work (only when other tracked activities are idle), outfit fitting where supported, and relevant health/armor purchases for food, bait, pharmacy, sports and tactical stores. Purchases do not charge when full, poorer than the price, or outside the original shop; food never strips existing armor. This expands useful counter interactions without pretending every shelf prop is collectible inventory. Existing original service behavior stays available.

## Rendering, collision and performance

- Faceted overlapping cloud spheres become shared soft-alpha textured planes (12 on Low, 20 otherwise), with original generated artwork and no new lights or asset downloads.
- One shared 128px grayscale detail texture adds subtle ground grain in the existing world-ground shader. It retains the original world color map, elevations, roads and footprint; no added scenery objects or gameplay colliders. This cannot by itself fill undeveloped parcels or prove the old full-city screenshot issue is resolved.
- Terrain-following asphalt now has bounded 512-unit frustum-culling chunks rather than one city-wide draw volume. Cell selection/support sampling and tile grid remain unchanged. Trade-off: more possible draw calls, fewer submitted offscreen road triangles; no claimed measured FPS improvement without device tests.
- Adaptive resolution clears stale slow-frame history in menus/hidden tabs rather than reducing resolution based on paused time. Existing desktop/mobile quality and shadow budgets are retained.
- Pedestrian vehicle movement also sweeps static walls and safely handles already-reached points/uninitialized animation state. Story walkers use the same vehicle-aware path steps. Nearby residents use a spatial neighbor index for personal space; business customers check peers, cars and swept furniture paths. This does not implement traffic lights, driving AI, vehicle-to-pedestrian crash damage or universal story crowd avoidance for scripted parade formations.
