# Bayou Laugh Theater reference auditorium

Damion's existing Bayou Laugh Theater gets a red-and-gold auditorium inspired by the supplied ornate and intimate theaters. The actual 160×128 room holds 50 modeled plush red seats with wooden shells/arms, a clear central carpet aisle with gold edging, a gold/cream proscenium, pleated open curtains and valance, cream pilasters and inset red wall panels with scroll motifs, warm sconces, scenic balcony box fronts, an oval ceiling medallion/rosettes and a tiered candle-style chandelier.

The original stage dimensions, Damion actor identity/position/elevation, microphone and show/dialogue behavior remain. The old six generic chair blocks are replaced by auditorium seats; up to six original audience actors are seated in the new layout, with no population increase. Generic furnishing/register/finish/business stock is excluded for the curated theater only. The main door, service point, parcel/frontage and other story venues are unchanged.

Balcony-style railings and fascia are scenery, not new traversable floors, stairs or occupancy. The existing low roof/room height remains; the ornate ceiling is a procedural interpretation within that envelope, not the full architectural scale of the photo references. Chandelier and sconces use self-lit materials without adding scene lights or shadow maps. Original signage and modeled ornament avoid copied/watermarked photo assets.

`/game/theater-review.html` renders the production stage/theater builders in an isolated room with a simplified actor fixture. Cameras show the auditorium, reverse stage view and ceiling. It is not full-world gameplay, a live performance/audio test or physical-device performance evidence.

Focused tests cover a radius-ten entrance/service-to-stage aisle in several footprints, seat-collider bounds, finite instanced geometry below the original roof, draw budgets/no real lights, production story integration and unchanged Damion identity/stage coordinates. Existing city-story and interior-system tests also run.
