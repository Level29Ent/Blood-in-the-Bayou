# Greenmart fresh-market interior

Photo-inspired ground floor: wooden produce islands with piled fruit and price boards, three five-tier double-sided packaged-goods racks with shelf-edge price strips, back-wall glass-front cold cases, green/wood checkout counters with belts and screens, polished concrete-look flooring, dark exposed ceiling/ducts and long strip fixtures. Small original package textures and deterministic per-instance color variation suggest groceries without downloading branded images. Upper-floor shelves retain home/apparel/electronics use and gain stocked displays and the architectural finish.

The existing parcel, 48-unit entrance, checkout interaction, cash prices, prologue/Anthony mission points and two lift positions remain unchanged. `greenmart-interior.js` shares fixture footprints with the live collision system. A radius-10 flood fill reaches checkout, both lifts and all four longitudinal aisle lanes on both floors. Upper-floor collision and height, purchase/cash handling, and zombie exclusion continue to use the existing Greenmart implementation.

The referenceInterior flag suppresses legacy retail-stock/business-richness decorations only for this rebuilt store; their purchase UI and other businesses are unchanged. Ground and upper detail groups render only for the active floor within 550 units of the store center. Geometry is instanced; textures are three original 128-square maps per floor, with no GLBs, external requests, dynamic lights or shadow maps added. This is an allocation bound, not a device FPS claim.

`/game/greenmart-review.html` builds the production Greenmart shell, fixtures and service hooks in an isolated scene at the real parcel. Cameras cover produce, aisles and cold cases. Front-lift and checkout buttons invoke real service hooks using fictional fixture cash/health. It excludes the full city and later mission/retail wrappers, so it does not verify full-world startup, live mission handoffs or phone performance. The iOS download contains refreshed sources, not a signed/compiled app.
