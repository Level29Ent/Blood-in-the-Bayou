# Sheldon’s Threads, character polish and farm corrections

## Walk-in clothing store
An existing ordinary business footprint becomes **Sheldon’s Threads**, retaining its street-facing door and protected service aisle. **CITY STORIES → SHELDON’S THREADS** sets directions. Inside, U / USE opens the fitting panel with the supplied Sheldon portrait and a procedural avatar inspired by the silver durag, facial hair, black shirt and gold jewelry. This is stylized game art, not a scanned or photorealistic likeness.

The catalog contains exactly **100 visual options**: five cuts/styles × four colors × five categories (tops, trousers, shoes, headwear, accessories). They are not 100 imported garment meshes. Select one item per category; selections immediately update the in-engine fitting avatar and persist in browser-local storage. Rotate the fitting preview or reset the outfit. Customization is free; no real-money checkout or game-cash charge is introduced.

The fitting panel’s **WEAR IT · OUTFIT VIEW / FIRST PERSON** controls also work on touch devices. Press **C on foot** to see the dressed local avatar with an outfit-view camera; C returns to first person. Existing vehicle C-camera behavior remains. First-person weapons/hands are unchanged. Jojo’s cutscene now uses the selected outfit on its player figure. Multiplayer outfit synchronization is not included. No account/cloud wardrobe syncing is claimed.

## Characters
Bonnie’s former ~39-unit model was nearly double ordinary humanoid height; its root is now scaled to .54 (about 21 units). Her existing braid/dress silhouette is retained, with added eyes, earrings, mouth and buttons. Business/civilian figures gain lightweight cloth weave and seam/name-patch detail. Named figures gain finer tailoring; Sheldon gets a durag, facial hair, earrings and jewelry. These remain procedural models, not external asset replacements.

## Farm ground and equipment
The farm tile grid already marked grass but could retain an old **decorative basketball feature** that painted a court and built hoops over the same field. Overlapping legacy decorative court features are now retired before rendering. The separately reserved playable recreation court is excluded by the allocator and remains intact. An additional farm/amenity overlap assertion and final textured grass paint keep the agricultural field distinct. Farm roads continue around the perimeter; there is no new through-road.

The existing barn remains; the farm gains an exterior farmhouse with pitched roof/windows/porch, a tractor with tires/hubs/exhaust, hay bales and a water trough. The farmhouse/tractor have collision footprints and do not occupy the animal rows or entrance lane. The farmhouse is exterior scenery, not a new enterable residence.

Cows/pigs gain limited leg motion and mild size variation; chickens gain layered wing feathers. Shared hide textures and existing rounded anatomy remain, and static parts stay material-batched. This is bounded procedural animation, not new photorealistic/skinned animal assets.

## Limits
Physical-iPhone/Safari FPS, precise portrait likeness, new multiplayer garment replication and full unassisted playthroughs remain unverified. Fixed low/direct graphics, transparent glass and quiet rain are not changed. Existing Next.js security upgrade remains separate.
