# NOLA expansion — remaining activity mechanics

This follow-up replaces the first-release limitations with playable versions of the requested systems. They use existing premises, procedural models and the game's current physics/combat. They are not newly photorealistic buildings, cinematic animation sets, a second open-world map, or a claim of live-device verification.

## Access and controls

Use **Pause → NOLA BUSINESSES / CITY MAP**, then visit the marked service approach. Existing first-release services remain. **E** default interaction / **G** vehicle controls (and legacy U/E), 1–4 slots, touch movement and third-person C remain; **Tab** while playing opens the city map. Tab inside menus still traverses controls. The added corner map is a keyboard-focusable/tappable route-map control.

## Compatibility, saves and failure paths

All defaults remain enabled for everyone going forward. No retroactive billing or deductions. Crafted/consumable ownership sits under `nola.activities` in the same campaign snapshot as cash; Continue/Save Code carry it. Current wagers, driver entities, crew and defense do not resume after load. Per-mission and hourly terms are disclosed before hiring. No infinite payouts, simulated offline money or negative payroll balances.

Paid activities exclude overlapping campaign, career, guard, neighborhood, enterprise, street, heist and survivor-story starts. Existing ready/busy gates have optional activity checks, preserving behavior when this module is absent. Scope/vehicle camera precedence remains; third-person yields to spectator camera context. Existing map/door/road data is not rewritten by these modules. A failed safe vehicle berth/road route refuses the operation rather than destroying protected lots or teleporting a car.

The activity presentation and balancing are deliberately straightforward. Models use existing procedural figure/gator/vehicle builders with original attachments; pit is an instanced encounter location rather than a new exterior building. Defenders attack through the existing simple cooldown/HP AI, not motion-captured wrestling. Driver is conservative and may stop at hard corners/blocked routes; manual takeover remains available. Pyro/flame are bounded gameplay effects, not fluid/combustion simulation.

## Validation and unverified work

Pure-core and VM runtime tests cover route connectivity/edge wrapping/remote snap/one-clearance-check-per-node, bounded wager stakes and one-time win/loss/tie settlement, actual modeled bout lifecycle/forfeit/return cleanup, driver inputs/blocked braking/manual takeover without teleport, ferry hull loss/success payout, hourly payroll/no overdraft, ownership-only snapshots/Continue composition, finite fuel/LOS damage and cooldown, real tool/ferry/float geometry and viewmodel visibility, moving pyro inventory/cleanup, no audio auto-unlock and muted gains, and startup/source ordering. Existing foundation, wardrobe/third-person, prologue, mission, road and economy suites remain.

Passing tests and build are **not** a visual full-city playthrough or proof of phone/GPU performance, generated-world counter/berth availability, every driver route or combat feel. Those checks require a reachable preview; the PR records exact deployment state. No screenshot or successful live/device validation is claimed without that evidence. No extra real-time lights, external dependencies or new production interval loops introduced. New scenery/projectile resources are bounded and cleaned up at activity/world transitions.
