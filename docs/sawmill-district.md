# Sawmill rear business district and graded street rendering

The sawmill venue is the anchor; rear direction is opposite its actual frontage, not an assumed map compass direction. Add up to four named ordinary businesses on verified vacant grass/park behind it within 1152 world units: Blackwater Workers Cafe, Mill Road Neighborhood Market, Blackwater Auto Repair and Mill Road Contractors Office. They reuse existing cafe meal, grocery, repair and business service flows, not invented stock/fees. No replacement of existing named venues or lots.

Allocation reserves buildings, activity/landmark parcels, existing foundation pads, roads and water. Each new parcel requires sampled low, near-flat ground and an obstruction-free apron with a 96-unit-wide dry route to an existing road. Subsequent parcels reserve earlier roads and approaches. Unavailable parcels are skipped rather than bulldozed, remotely relocated or placed across water. Added grid footprints remain lots rather than solid building tiles so existing venue wall/door/furniture collision supports entering them. Generic authored builders/services retain responsibility for equipment and pricing. No account/billing changes or production deploy.

The terrain rendering investigation found a reproducible asphalt omission: street-surfaces rejected all tiles with more than two units of grade, including valid sloped roads. It now covers finite road grades up to sixteen units per 32-unit tile while rejecting cliffs/nonfinite heights, water and occupied footprints. Graded tiles use eight-unit terrain samples so the asphalt follows support rather than cutting a single flat triangle through it. Flat roads retain six vertices and original UV/material behavior; no global 16× geometry expansion. This addresses missing graded asphalt, not proof that every empty field or other artifact in the supplied screenshot had this cause. No draw-distance expansion, population increase or lighting changes.

Tests cover all four sawmill frontage directions, deterministic placement, protected parcels, connected dry roads, no-road/water/high-ground no-op behavior, actual generation registration, tile-apron alignment, and graded asphalt support/flat-road budget.
