# City Stories: Major Cajun, AK Gun Plus, Slab Motors and Bayou Laugh Theater

Open **CITY STORIES** beneath the status HUD to set a destination, then meet each owner inside the building. These are single-player side stories: finish Ronniebaby's active story mission or paid shift first. Campaign progress is not advanced by these quests. Existing footprints/doors are reused; there is no new mountain or farm construction.

## Locations and characters

- **Major Cajun** replaces the fish market at world X=2624, Y=1728, the downtown Esplanade/Tchoupitoulas area. Other fish markets remain. Chief Jojo has a white chef's jacket, black buttons, striped dark trousers, dark hair, toque and wooden spoon, inspired by the supplied photo.
- **AK Gun Plus** reuses the downtown community-business footprint at X=2176, Y=1728. Alex has a blue-gray hoodie, brown hair and beard inspired by the third attachment. The standard armory remains playable, with a separate story/experimental-weapon service inside.
- There was no dedicated theater interior in this checkout. The screen-test venue at X=2592, Y=2016 is converted to **Bayou Laugh Theater**, with a stage, microphone, seated audience and glasses/olive-shirt/light-trouser Damion inspired by the fourth attachment.
- These are stylized procedural game characters, not scanned or photorealistic reproductions. Optimized versions of the three supplied portraits appear in their conversation panels. No identity, voice, or biographical facts are inferred from the images.

## Jojo: Recipe Gone Wrong

1. Talk to Chief Jojo inside Major Cajun and accept Zombie Gumbo.
2. Follow the gold story marker for rice at the farmers market, pork at the farm, shrimp at the Natchez steamboat dock, fresh alligator meat from a player kill, and roux from Ronniebaby in the mansion penthouse. The first three collection sites spawn infected encounters; clear those guards before holding **U / STORY ACTION** to collect. Alligator combat uses existing wildlife damage logic; no meat is granted just by reaching the bayou. Take the mansion elevator for the roux.
3. Return the ingredients. Weaken the marked university subject below half health, then hold the action button nearby to capture it alive. If killed by the player, another subject is marked. Capturable subjects cannot be permanently lost to ambient/fortress guard kills.
4. Escort the captive back; it follows visibly on foot and travels as restrained cargo in a vehicle.
5. A 40-second in-engine cinematic uses the restaurant kitchen, Jojo, a representative player humanoid and chained captive. It shows preparation/stirring, boiling blue-to-brown liquid, ice and ladle/bowl serving, the requested gumbo dialogue, exactly five seconds of violent convulsions, stillness, broken chains, enlarged mutation and an exit. No additional postprocessing or video download is used. **SKIP SCENE** advances to the boss fight rather than granting completion.
6. Control returns outside with brief protection. Kill the 260-HP Gumbo Behemoth to earn $6,500 in fictional cash. Jojo's recipe/completion is saved locally; an interrupted live capture/scene/boss encounter resumes from capture dispatch rather than serializing live entities.

## Alex: timed weapons and field research

- **Arc Broom ($450)** and **Cajun Thunder ($650)** use earned game cash only. Each lasts **30 wall-clock seconds**, including menus. They have distinct projectile damage, penetration, cooldowns and colored experimental models. The previous selected owned weapon returns at expiry; temporary weapons are not written into permanent inventory.
- Five-wave field trials supply a free temporary weapon. Clear each wave to advance; wave five contains brutes. Completing all five pays $4,200.
- Research requires humane live capture of a farm cow, pig and chicken, followed by three marked brute/boss-specimen captures at progressively harder locations. Weaken boss specimens below 35% HP and hold nearby; killing one forces a replacement, not a successful capture. Return to Alex with all specimens for $5,000. Farm animals are temporarily hidden while transported and released on completion/abandonment. This is a stylized capture interaction, not an animal breeding/cargo simulation.
- Ordinary permanent-weapon armories remain elsewhere. No Stripe, Apple purchase or paid cosmetic integration is introduced.

## Bonnie: three recovery contracts

Talk inside Slab Motors (or choose **RECOVERY MISSIONS** in her sales panel). Recover the **exact marked sedan, sports car and Houston slab** and drive each to Bonnie's marked return area. A purchased or random replacement is not accepted. Return at least 25% vehicle health; repair damaged vehicles first. Destroying the contract vehicle requires restarting. Complete all three for $4,800. Only completion count is saved; live vehicles are not restored across reloads.

## Damion: jokes and audio

Entering the theater triggers original fictional comedy captions. Enable **PERFORMANCE AUDIO** in Damion's panel for a generic device/browser speech voice. Jojo's scene has the same opt-in generic dialogue option. **No recorded performance was supplied, and no photographed person's voice is cloned or imitated.** Browser speech support, installed voices, autoplay and mute settings vary; captions remain the fallback. Speech stops on leaving the theater, mute, blur, page hide or scene completion. Speech is not sample-accurately lip-synced.

## Casino correction and limitations

BoomCity's facade now follows its venue's final position/orientation, rather than always using the old fixed shell coordinates. The redundant doorway-blocking panel is removed and neon uprights stay at the edges. Signs have larger sRGB mipmapped canvases, linear filtering and a modest depth offset to reduce shimmer/z-fighting. This does not claim that every pixel artifact in a physical Safari/GPU capture is eliminated.

The game stays on the existing fixed lightweight renderer, with transparent glass and no tone mapping. Character and animation fidelity remains procedural. Multiplayer versions of the new quests/rentals are deliberately disabled; existing multiplayer gameplay is unchanged. The pre-existing Next.js security advisory remains outstanding.
