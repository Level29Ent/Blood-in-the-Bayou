Generic home/clinic clutter and the old career-specific equipment are skipped only when the authored vet model has been built. Other clinics/hospitals remain untouched. No new animals, treatment minigame, purchases, NPCs or room transitions are introduced. Kennels are empty decorative recovery bays, not interactive cages.

Radius-10 route tests reach the unchanged center service point, treatment aisle and reception/seating approaches. Stainless furnishings are original procedural geometry, with 21 instanced batches/four 128-square maps and no GLB/image downloads, dynamic lights, render targets, shadow maps or new animation loops. Allocation bounds are not phone FPS measurements. `/game/vet-clinic-review.html` shares the production model at actual room size within a matching simple shell, not the full city. Full-city traversal and live service/contract flow require separate acceptance checks. Native source archive refreshed, not compiled/signed. Stylized interpretation, not photoreal reconstruction.
