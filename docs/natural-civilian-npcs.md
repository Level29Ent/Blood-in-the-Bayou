# More natural everyday civilian NPCs

The street-resident/festival crowd and explicitly styled business customers receive a focused procedural-art refinement inspired by the supplied mixed everyday crowd references, not copies of pictured people. Smaller eye apertures/nose/lip volumes, better head height/width balance, restrained footwear, less outstretched neutral arms and four stable male build widths reduce toy-like silhouettes. Existing mixed skin tones, eight hairstyle sets including long hair/locs, adult women/men and clothing identities remain. Ordinary clothing now draws from twelve muted/neutral/accent colors with deterministic distribution; festival purple/gold/green stays.

The existing shirt crowd batch gains collar, hem, front seam and fuller sleeves/shoulders. Detailed business customers explicitly styled through `styleResident` get aligned head/face proportions, reduced eye/mouth volume, closer shoulder spacing and a shared shirt-detail geometry using the actor's existing shirt material. Repeated styling is idempotent, keeps body/limb rig handles and identity colors, and doesn't allocate geometry per customer.

Named character factories and infected rigs are not opted into resident reshaping. No universal replacement of Ronniebaby, Captain Smith, story characters, soldiers or zombies. No new GLB/photo-scan assets, copied faces, uniforms, NPC population, collision/AI/pathfinding, animation timings, missions or physics changes. Crowd standing/walking/cheer motions use original transforms with adjusted neutral shoulder spacing only. Full customer rigs preserve existing animation hierarchy. This improves stylized game people; it is not photorealism, facial animation or a full articulated animation overhaul.

Crowd remains nine instanced draw batches at existing population/capacity, with extra facial/clothing vertices merged into them. No new texture downloads, dependency, real-time light or production animation loop. Existing skin/cloth shared texture cache retained. Detailed customer extra shirt cues share geometry and are incorporated by the existing business static-part merging path.

`/game/natural-residents-review.html` loads actual production appearance and crowd code with group/close-up/standing-walking views. It is a model review, not full-city AI, detailed customer animation or physical mobile-GPU verification. Tests cover civilian opt-in/named/infected isolation, stable identity/colors/idempotence, finite animated rig matrices, geometry reuse and nine-batch mixed crowds at capacity, plus existing women/hair/festival/zombie regressions. A deployed visual review and device testing remain necessary.
